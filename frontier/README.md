# frontier, a commons of what works

> A frontier is just this JSON file plus something that renders it. Run your own
> for your team or company, or contribute to the public one. See
> [../docs/federation.md](../docs/federation.md).

`reference-frontier.json` is where the field gets better together. It isn't a
scoreboard. It's a living collection of techniques that turn tokens into durable
work, each carried with the evidence that it worked. When you run `vibrant-tune`,
this is what lets it say "setups like yours that tried this saw that," pointing at
something someone actually did rather than an opinion.

## The flywheel

You bring your wins to the commons, and you take home everyone else's. The moment
one setup finds a breakthrough, the technique behind it lands here, and on
everyone's next run it's on the menu, matched to setups like theirs. Someone
speeds a workflow tenfold, and the pattern is waiting for the rest of us.
Generosity is the growth engine, and the frontier rises as anyone rises.

## Contributing (opt-in, anonymized)

Run `vibrant-report` or `vibrant-tune`, let it draft an entry, and open a PR. An entry
carries the part that transfers:

- the **technique**, the engine configuration that produced the result, described
  so someone else can adopt it,
- the **fingerprint**: engine class, model roles, effort, review regime,
- the **efficiency vector**, the survival **horizon**, and the sample size,
- a date.

It carries nothing that identifies you: no name, no repo names, no code, no
product information. The commons compares technique and results, never people, and
never against product outcomes. That's the governance line (`docs/governance.md`).
Bring only your own wins, and only with your own consent.

The seed entries are from one operator's Claude Code logs at the same-session
survival floor (Aug 2026), a starting point rather than a verdict. Better data,
especially at day and week horizons and from other harnesses, is what we're hoping
you'll add.
