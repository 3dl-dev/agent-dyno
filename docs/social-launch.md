# Vibrant launch campaign: Tokenmaxxing 2.0

Positioning: **Tokenmaxxing 2.0. Progress above activity.**

## The story in one line

Tokenmaxxing 1.0 was burning as many tokens as possible, rolling coal to look busy.
2.0 is the opposite: the most surviving work per token. Spend big if the job needs it,
but haul the cargo to match.

## Key messages

- **Progress above activity.** We measure what your AI coding setup ships and keeps in git,
  per token, not how many tokens it burns.
- **Bragging rights, earned.** Surviving work per token, harness-neutral, read from your own
  logs and git history. A vector, not a single grade: the winner on one axis rarely wins
  another.
- **Supportive, not just a scoreboard.** The report hands you the one change most likely to
  move your number, for your usage.
- **Own your data.** It runs on your machine. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share,
  and a shared result is an anonymized roll-up, never your code or identity.
- **Guardrail (keep every post on this side of it):** it ranks engine craft and technique,
  never individuals, and never against product outcomes. Copy talks about setups, not people.

## Assets

- Landing / shareable card: `https://vibrant.3dl.dev`
- Your own card (after a run, nothing uploaded, the four numbers ride in the link):
  `https://vibrant.3dl.dev/#you=<engine>,<cost>,<eff>,<waste>,<cache>`
- Social unfurl image: `docs/assets/og.png` (1280x640, under 1MB). GitHub repo card needs a
  manual re-upload at Settings > Social preview (no API); the site unfurl updates on deploy.
- The tool: `/plugin marketplace add 3dl-dev/vibrant`, `/plugin install vibrant@vibrant`,
  then ask "How efficient is my coding setup?" Or clone the repo and run Claude Code in it.
